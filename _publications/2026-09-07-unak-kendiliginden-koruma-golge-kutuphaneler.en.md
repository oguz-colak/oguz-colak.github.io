This paper will be presented at the ÜNAK '26 Symposium on 7–8 September 2026. The study conceptually addresses the alternative preservation function of shadow libraries in terms of sustainability and accessibility of digital content.

### Abstract
In the digital preservation literature, the position and functions of alternative preservation mechanisms created by shadow libraries within the ecosystem are discussed alongside formal infrastructures.

### Key Topics
*   Shadow libraries and digital preservation.
*   Alternative preservation infrastructures.
*   Strategic position of information centers.
