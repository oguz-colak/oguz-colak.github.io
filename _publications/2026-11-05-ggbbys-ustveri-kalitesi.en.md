This paper was presented at the V. From Tradition to Future Information and Records Management Symposium on 5–6 November 2026. The study comparatively examines metadata quality in library and information science journals in Türkiye through national publishing platforms and OpenAlex.

### Abstract
The quality of metadata produced in academic journals plays a determining role in the discovery of scholarly content and in scholarly communication processes. Within this framework, metadata records on national publishing platforms and open indexes such as OpenAlex are compared, and the dimensions of metadata quality are discussed.

### Key Topics
*   Metadata and metadata quality.
*   Library and information science journals.
*   National publishing platforms and OpenAlex.
